# Phase H/I Runtime Closeout

## H — Continuous Trust
Runtime trust is treated as a hard dependency of autonomous execution. Tenant canaries, billing liveness, artifact verification, incident regression, intelligence safety and remediation evidence must remain healthy before trust can propagate downstream.

## I — Autonomous Governance & BI
Governance and BI evidence feed risk budgets, decision quality, anomaly correlation, human overrides and governed scenarios. The autonomy gate must reject execution when trust, evidence quality, risk budget or drift safety is invalid.

## Downstream rule
H/I do not directly enable production. They provide evidence consumed by K/L autonomy and M production certification. Any missing or stale evidence remains fail-closed.
